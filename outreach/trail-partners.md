# Trail data partners

Neither HIKEArmenia nor AllTrails lets other sites reuse their trails without permission.
These are the requests to send. Until they answer, AllHikes draws trails from OpenStreetMap.

---

## HIKEArmenia

**To:** info@hikearmenia.org
**Subject:** Showing HIKEArmenia trails on AllHikes, a calendar of group hikes in Armenia

Hello HIKEArmenia team,

I'm Anna Ter-Kazarian. I run yerevent.club, a calendar of events in Armenia, and I'm building
AllHikes: one website that lists every upcoming group hike in Armenia from more than 30 clubs,
in English, Armenian and Russian.

Many of these club hikes follow trails you have already mapped and described. We'd love to
show your route on the map next to each matching hike, with your name, a link to the trail on
hikearmenia.org and to your app. It would send hikers your way and help tourists find
both the trail and a group to walk it with.

Would you be open to this? Ideally we'd use your trail routes (GPX or similar) and names,
always credited and linked to you. We're happy to follow any conditions you set.

Thank you, and thank you for everything you've done for hiking in Armenia.

Anna Ter-Kazarian
AllHikes · yerevent.club

---

## AllTrails

**To:** support@alltrails.com (ask them to forward to partnerships)
**Subject:** Partnership request: linking Armenian group hikes to AllTrails trails

Hello,

I'm Anna Ter-Kazarian, founder of AllHikes, a website that lists every upcoming group hike
run by hiking clubs in Armenia (about 90 a month, from 30+ clubs), in three languages.

We'd like to link each hike to the matching AllTrails trail page, or show the trail on our
map, so hikers can see the route, reviews and photos before they book. Your terms ask for
written permission before linking to or reusing AllTrails content, so we're asking first.

Is there a partner program, embed or API we could use? We'd credit AllTrails clearly on
every trail.

Thank you,

Anna Ter-Kazarian
AllHikes
