# AllHikes

Hiking-tour aggregator for Armenia: a separate brand from yerevent. The owner (Anna) is
non-technical, so explain things in plain words.

## Layout

- `assets/app.js`: every page's behaviour and all UI text (en/hy/ru, the `T` object). Each
  page's `<body>` says which view it is (`data-page="home" | "clubs" | "club"`) and the path
  back to the site root (`data-root`), so links work both at a sub-path and on a custom domain.
- `assets/style.css`: the look (tokens at the top, light and dark).
- `assets/logos/<instagram handle>.jpg`: club logos, 160px (`scripts/fetch_logos.py`).
- Generated by `scripts/build.py`, don't hand-edit: `assets/data.js`, `index.html`,
  `clubs/index.html`, `clubs/<slug>/index.html`.
- `data/`: `hikes-raw.json` (Esora), `hikes-ig.json` (Instagram via Bright Data + Claude),
  `clubs.json` (the club list and contacts; set `active: false` to hide one),
  `trails.json` + `places.json` (OpenStreetMap). Don't hand-edit the generated ones.

## Rules worth keeping

- "big" listings (3+ days, or more than 60 days away) go to the Ararat table and big-trips
  section. Their prices under 6,000 are dollars even though Esora says AMD.
- Difficulty: the club's own words first, otherwise estimated from km and climb, marked "~".
- Map pins come from matching the hike's name to OpenStreetMap places; trails are OSM
  `route=hiking` relations within 2.5 km of the pin or named in the hike. Credit
  "© OpenStreetMap contributors" wherever maps or trails appear.
- AllTrails forbids scraping, copying and embedding its trails; HIKEArmenia has no licence or
  API. Don't use either source's data without written permission.
- Browsers often have no Armenian date names, so `fmtDate` builds Armenian dates by hand.
- Keep `noindex` in the page heads until Anna says clubs have agreed to be listed.

## Automatic jobs (.github/workflows)

- `update.yml`: daily, Esora then build. `instagram.yml`: weekly, needs secrets
  `BRIGHTDATA_TOKEN` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, capped at 600 Bright Data records a month.
  `trails.yml`: monthly OpenStreetMap refresh.

Check a change: `python scripts/build.py`, `python -m http.server 8767`, open it at phone
width (430px), and switch through all three languages on the home, clubs and one club page.
