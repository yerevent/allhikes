# AllHikes

Hiking-tour aggregator for Armenia: a separate brand from yerevent. The owner (Anna) is
non-technical, so explain things in plain words.

- `src/template.html` is the page: CSS, UI strings (en/hy/ru) and JS. The data goes in at
  `/*__DATA__*/[]` and the build date at `__UPDATED__`. Edit this file, not `index.html`.
- `index.html` is generated by `scripts/build.py` (and rebuilt daily by the GitHub Action).
  Don't hand-edit it.
- `data/hikes-raw.json` is the Esora pull. Don't hand-edit it.
- "big" listings (3+ days, or more than 60 days away) go to the Ararat table / big-trips
  section. Their prices under 6,000 are dollars even though Esora says AMD.
- Difficulty: the club's own words first; otherwise estimated from km and climb, marked with "~".
- Browsers often have no Armenian date names, so `fmtDate` builds Armenian dates by hand.
- Keep `noindex` until Anna says clubs have agreed to be listed.

Check a change: `python scripts/build.py`, `python -m http.server 8767`, open it at phone
width (430px), and switch through all three languages.
